How to approach these

  • Keep the movement relaxed and easy
  • Breathe through each rep to help your body settle
  • Pain isn't the goal — ease into range and build over time
  • Support your recovery by eating well, staying hydrated, sleeping well and managing stress as best you can

Reading intensity

Some exercises use RIR — reps in reserve. It's how many more reps you could have done when you stopped.

RIRWhat it felt like
3Comfortable, plenty left
2Working, but controlled
1Hard, one more at most
0Nothing left, form starting to go

Your target sits next to each exercise. If you log a higher number than the target, the field turns red — that means the set was easier than intended, so add a little load next time.

Mobility work uses Low or Mod instead. Low should feel easy and unforced. Mod is noticeable but never painful.

How this works

Use the dropdown at the top to move between weeks. Each exercise shows the sets, reps and intensity.

Some exercises are done more than once a week. Work through the sessions in order — the next one unlocks once you've logged the one before.

The badge next to each exercise shows how you're tracking against your first week. Tap it to see the number.

Notes are optional, but if something feels better, the same or worse, write it down.

Aim to have everything done 12 hours before our follow-up session.
