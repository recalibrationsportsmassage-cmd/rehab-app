How this works

Use the dropdown at the top to move between weeks. Each exercise shows the sets, reps and intensity.

For some exercises, we'll track intensity as Reps in Reserve (RIR) — how many reps you had left before failure. 0 means you couldn't have done another rep.

After each set, log your load, reps and intensity. The badge next to each exercise shows how you're tracking against your first week. Tap it to see the number.

Notes are optional, but if something feels better, the same or worse, write it down.

Aim to have everything done 12 hours before our follow-up session.
